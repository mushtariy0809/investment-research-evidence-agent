EXAMPLECO INC. — FORM 10-Q FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2024 — SYNTHETIC EVALUATION FIXTURE
PART I — FINANCIAL INFORMATION
Item 1. Financial Statements

The unaudited condensed consolidated statements of operations report total net revenue of $168.3 million for the three months ended June 30, 2024, compared to $147.9 million for the three months ended June 30, 2023. Subscription revenue was $135.1 million and Services revenue was $33.2 million for the quarter. Net income for the quarter was $17.2 million, compared to $13.4 million in the prior year period. Cash and cash equivalents were $231.8 million as of June 30, 2024. The Company generated $28.4 million of operating cash flow during the quarter. Deferred revenue increased to $204.3 million as of June 30, 2024. Basic earnings per share were $0.42 for the quarter compared to $0.33 in the prior year period.

Item 2. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Total net revenue for the second quarter of fiscal 2024 increased 14% year over year, driven primarily by continued growth in Subscription revenue, which increased 17% compared to the prior year quarter. The increase reflects both new customer additions and expansion within the existing customer base, where net revenue retention was 112%. Services revenue was approximately flat year over year. Gross margin for the quarter was 72.3%, an improvement of 90 basis points from the prior year quarter, driven by cloud infrastructure cost optimization. Operating expenses increased 9% year over year, primarily due to increased sales headcount in European markets. Management continues to expect full-year capital expenditures of approximately $18 million, principally for data analytics infrastructure. The Company launched its new demand-forecasting module during the quarter, which management believes will support subscription growth in the second half of the fiscal year.

PART II — OTHER INFORMATION
Item 1A. Risk Factors

The following risk factor supplements the risk factors disclosed in our Annual Report on Form 10-K for fiscal 2023. During the second quarter of fiscal 2024, a new data protection regulation was enacted in the European Union that will apply to certain of our analytics products beginning in 2025. Compliance may require changes to product architecture and additional legal and engineering expenses, and failure to comply could result in fines of up to 4% of annual revenue. In addition, we completed the migration of a substantial portion of our workloads to a second cloud infrastructure provider during the quarter, which reduces but does not eliminate our dependency on a single provider. The migration introduces transitional operational risk during the remainder of the fiscal year. There have been no other material changes to the risk factors previously disclosed in the Annual Report.
