NOVATECH SEMICONDUCTOR CORP. — FORM 10-K FOR FISCAL YEAR 2023 — SYNTHETIC EVALUATION FIXTURE
Item 1. Business

NovaTech Semiconductor Corp. designs analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits for the automotive, industrial, and consumer electronics markets. The automotive market is the Company’s largest end market, representing 52% of net revenue in fiscal 2023, followed by industrial at 31% and consumer at 17%. The Company is fabless and outsources all wafer fabrication to third-party foundries located primarily in Taiwan. NovaTech sells through a combination of direct sales and independent distributors; distributors accounted for approximately 58% of net revenue in fiscal 2023. The Company’s product portfolio includes power management circuits, sensor interfaces, and battery-management systems for electric vehicles. NovaTech employed approximately 3,200 people worldwide as of the end of fiscal 2023 and holds over 1,400 issued patents.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

We depend on a limited number of third-party foundries, located principally in Taiwan, for all of our wafer fabrication, and any disruption at those foundries — including from natural disasters, geopolitical tensions, or capacity constraints — could materially and adversely affect our ability to ship products. A significant portion of our revenue comes from the automotive market, which is cyclical and subject to extended design cycles; a slowdown in electric vehicle production would reduce demand for our battery-management products. Two distributors together accounted for approximately 29% of our net revenue in fiscal 2023, and the loss of either distributor could reduce our revenue. We face intense competition from larger analog semiconductor companies with greater manufacturing scale. Export control regulations affecting semiconductor products could restrict our ability to sell to certain customers. Our gross margins depend on foundry wafer pricing, which we do not control.

Item 7. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Net revenue decreased 6% to $1.42 billion in fiscal 2023 from $1.51 billion in fiscal 2022, primarily due to an inventory correction in the consumer electronics end market, partially offset by 11% growth in automotive revenue. Gross margin declined to 58.2% from 60.1%, reflecting higher foundry wafer costs and lower factory utilization at our test facilities. Operating income was $312.5 million, compared to $371.8 million in fiscal 2022. The Company recorded inventory write-downs of $24.3 million during the year, principally for consumer products. Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments totaled $890.4 million at year end. The Company returned $210.0 million to shareholders through dividends during fiscal 2023 and ended the year with no outstanding borrowings under its revolving credit facility.
